Cause is not an ageing memory that failed to expire: water10 stores no bubble history. It treats instantaneous production as two seconds of equilibrium, divides it by depth, and includes an almost unit crest term whenever lift is positive. Its own fixture H=0.1 m, depth=0.1 m, slope=.2, crest=1 gives rate=11.89/s and cover=0.99999999995. A gentle shallow crest therefore fills every hole of the bubble texture. The game capture clearly shows the bed between thin foam threads. Its log's shallow landed level averages about 0.5 m deep, with wave lifts reaching 0.68 m over the grid: a depth-only breaking test can trigger across this whole shallow region (those maxima/means are not a paired point sample).

Measured common-water polygon: 4,014,096 full-size pixels, excludes dry beach and sky, drawn on a viewed raw frame. Near-white in this dark scene is min(R,G,B)>=80, max-min<=30 (not display-white 255). First raw frame game 1.861%, foam off 6.374%, failed shore foam 59.052%. At threshold 90: game .914%, off 2.846%, failed foam 56.104%. Smooth reflected sky can also pass this colour test: these are reproducible appearance shares, not ground-truth material IDs. Bright foam >=120 with spread<=25: game .0095%, failed foam 50.944% (16-frame averages). This explains why using a white fixture misses the scene-scale failure. Analysis scripts/results are in `.testruns/water11`.

Correction: keep the old offshore foam; add foam only in a centimetre-depth contact band at an active positive crest, and on steep depth-limited breaking fronts. Remove the previous-frame maximum and the equilibrium-with-depth division. The model's slope onset .35..65 and contact decay depth .01 m are stated choices, not numbers fitted pixel by pixel. Union with crest foam has no boundary seam. Expected raw appearance share about 3-8% at threshold 80 (old off already 6.4% because of pale reflections), with added shore white area at most a few percent, against game's 1.9%; broad 59% slabs must disappear. This is a falsifiable prediction, not an in-game result.

Deliberate small-step limit: persistent seconds of bubble ageing/advection are NOT implemented by this correction. One previous wave frame cannot provide that history, especially in a frozen world. Do not claim realistic trailing foam complete. First judge active production in the game before adding a world-space history system. Wet-ground history is unchanged.
