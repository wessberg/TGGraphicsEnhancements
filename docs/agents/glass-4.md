## Physics and scope of the change

A physically smooth cylinder 27 cm across, walls 5 mm, IOR 1.5, camera 1.4 m from its centre and wall 40 cm behind its centre is the useful yardstick. Use the game's recorded vertical FOV 58.71551 degrees and render height 1084 (capture 3840 x 2160): `physics.py` computes the exact four Snell crossings. The jar spans about 183 render pixels, close to the ~170-190 render-pixel jars measured in the full game image. This distance is inferred from projected size, not a captured instance transform.

The strongly bending rim begins near impact parameter R-thickness: the last 3.7 percent of the radius, about 3.45 render pixels on each side (roughly 7 output pixels at 4K). There the hollow-side boundary approaches grazing/total internal reflection. The middle half is not a magnifying lens; small shifts are still physical. The real mesh is polygonal, and has a shaped shoulder and thick bottom: no claim that every shoulder or polygon-edge bend should vanish. Trial 15 uses those actual surfaces; it does not manufacture a perfectly round shell or change geometry to make the stripes pass.

The film removes 17.5 percent at each drawn wall: 0.825 squared = 0.680625 of the light remains before Fresnel losses. It changes contrast amplitude and adds its own lit colour, not ray direction. Four near-normal 4 percent Fresnel losses give about 0.578 transmission on the direct path including the film; multiple reflections supply further paths. The rough reflection keeps its material finish. No denoiser or guide was changed.
