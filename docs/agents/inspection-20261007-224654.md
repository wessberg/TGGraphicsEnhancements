## A. What breaks the session itself

1. **Path tracing takes over 100 s to come on.** In the first scene both "still" traced steps (50 s each) show the
   game's own picture (luma 15.7, the game's flicker): the tracer was not composing yet. The merge worker's test took
   172 s to make its shader passes. (The owner: PT-74.)
2. **Video memory ran out** (12.92 of 13.39 GB): TG Better Upscaling stopped FSR and XeSS for the session and "DLSS Ray
   Reconstruction isn't denoising". Every step labelled RR after that (forest, vista) ran another denoiser (the report's
   table says NRD). The tracer's own share doubled against the last build (table).
   **And this is why the vista looks like a comic book** (the owner, 23:05, of the shield at the right of
   `vista/3-traced-rr/full.jpg`: "looked like a comic book"; `vista/crop-shield.jpg`: flat orange blocks, no wood). The
   log: "Shared textures (PT-45): 3243 of the traced scene's 4095 textures opened by the tracer with no copy ...; the
   others: 869 kept back near this card's video memory budget (their atlas tiles serve)". 869 textures were read as
   64x64 stand-in tiles. The shield, the vista's textureless ground (10), the plastic rocks (12) and the plain bridge
   floor (11) are probably this one cause, not the material programs: to tell apart by the yardstick's lines. The
   tracer's memory only grew through the session (252, 498, 974, 1,583, 2,166 MB): a left scene's data is not released.
   The coordinator had called the shield "fine" (a wheel); GPT-6-astra's list had it (B18).
3. **The tracer is much slower**: 5 times in the tavern (12 to 64 ms), a third more outdoors.
4. `candle@quick` never ran (the coordinator named the scene twice; the second recipe replaced the first): wax and skin
   scattering were not looked at by candle light.
